Sonic Boom: 6 hearts (12 health points) on one server tick.
The existing trueDamage helper triples its amount. Therefore Sonic Boom passes 4 raw points to produce exactly 12 points (6 hearts) of direct health loss.
This occurs immediately on activation, not across the 14-tick visual animation.
PvP must be enabled. Armour and Protection IV do not reduce direct health subtraction.
Not compiled or gameplay-tested in Minecraft.
